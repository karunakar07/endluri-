ABOUT

An engineer and mentor working at the intersection of research and public awareness

Karunakar Endluri has worked with QIS College of Engineering and Technology, AICTE IDEA LAB, and CDAC, with published work spanning ARM Cortex-M microcontrollers, embedded security systems, and applied electronics. He serves as a mentor with the Indian National Academy of Engineering.

Alongside his technical work, he runs cyber safety awareness workshops for schools and colleges — combining a pledge on cyber hygiene, a topic quiz, certificates, and free books for participating students.
